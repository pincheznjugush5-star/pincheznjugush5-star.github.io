Privacy Policy for Safari Puzzle

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

Safari Puzzle is an offline-capable mobile puzzle game that offers optional Google Cloud Syncing and optional rewarded video advertisements when connected to the internet.

1. Optional Google Sign-In & Cloud Syncing

You can play Safari Puzzle completely offline without signing in. If you choose to sign in with your Google Account inside the app's Settings menu to enable Google Cloud Syncing, we access and store only the information needed to back up and restore your game progress across devices:

  • Account Identifier: Your basic Google account identifier (such as your Google user ID and email address) used strictly to link your cloud save profile.
  • Game Progress Data: Your unlocked levels, star ratings, coins, gems, boosters, Wildlife Encyclopedia discoveries, Safari Reserve progress, and daily check-in status.

We do not sell, rent, or share your Google account details or game save data with third-party marketers.

2. Third-Party Rewarded Advertisements (Google AdMob)

In ad-supported store releases (such as Google Play, OPPO App Market, and Amazon Appstore), Safari Puzzle uses Google AdMob to serve optional rewarded video ads when you are online. Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and basic ad interaction data to deliver and measure advertisements. You can review Google's Privacy Policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Offline Play

If you do not sign in to Google Cloud Syncing and play offline, all game progress is stored locally on your device and no network requests or data transmissions occur.

4. Signing Out & Data Deletion

You may sign out of Google Cloud Syncing at any time from the Settings screen inside Safari Puzzle, which immediately stops cloud synchronization on that device. You can also revoke Safari Puzzle's access at any time from your Google Account permissions settings (myaccount.google.com/permissions) or request permanent deletion of your cloud save data by emailing us at the address below.
