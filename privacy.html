Privacy Policy for Safari Puzzle

Effective Date: November 22, 2026

Safari Puzzle is an offline-capable mobile puzzle game with optional rewarded video advertisements when connected to the internet.

1. Information Collection & Third-Party Ads

We do not directly collect personal account information. When online, Safari Puzzle uses Google AdMob to serve optional rewarded ads. Google AdMob may automatically collect device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID), IP address, and basic ad interaction data to deliver and measure advertisements.

2. Offline Play

When played offline, no network requests or data transmissions occur.
